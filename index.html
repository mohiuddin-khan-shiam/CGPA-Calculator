How CGPA & GPA Are Calculated

Official mathematical formulas for weighted cumulative grade point averages.

1. Semester GPA Formula

Your term GPA is the credit-weighted average of your semester's course grades:

Semester GPA = Σ (Course GPA × Course Credits) / Σ (Course Credits)

Example: (4.0 × 3 + 3.0 × 3) / (3 + 3) = 21 / 6 = 3.50

2. Updated Cumulative CGPA

Your updated CGPA blends past grade points with the new semester's quality points:

Updated CGPA = [ (Prior CGPA × Prior Credits) + Term Points ] / (Prior Credits + Term Credits)

Every credit hour carries equal weight toward your overall degree transcript.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to questions students ask every semester.

Will this CGPA calculation match my university transcript? ▾

Yes, this tool strictly follows the standard credit-weighted quality points formula used across accredited institutions worldwide. Note that some universities truncate decimals (e.g., 3.666 becomes 3.66) while others round up to two decimal places (3.67). We provide standard mathematical rounding to two decimal places.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA? ▾

GPA (Grade Point Average) measures academic performance for a single academic term or semester. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the cumulative average of all semesters completed throughout your entire degree program.

What happens if I retake a course? ▾

Course repeat policies vary by university:

  • Grade Replacement: The new grade completely replaces the old grade in CGPA calculations (total completed credits remain unchanged).
  • Grade Averaging: Both attempts count toward cumulative points and completed credits.

If your school uses grade replacement, adjust your "Current CGPA" and "Completed Credits" to subtract the prior attempt before adding the retaken course.

Is my academic data secure and private? ▾

100% yes. No course names, grades, or personal details are ever uploaded to a server or third party. Everything is computed instantly in your browser's local memory.

Can I print or save a projection report? ▾

Yes! Click the Print Report button on the calculator sidebar or press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on macOS). The site includes a dedicated print stylesheet that formats your courses, semester GPA, and cumulative projection into a clean document ready for advising meetings.
